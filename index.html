FAQ

Fair questions,
straight answers.

Does it really need no internet?

Really. The MediaPipe pose model is bundled inside the APK and inference runs on your phone's GPU. Airplane mode changes nothing — open it, prop it, plank.

How does it judge my form?

It tracks 33 body landmarks every frame and measures what a coach measures: body angle relative to the floor, hip line, knee bend. Cross the thresholds and you hear about it immediately.

What exactly stops the timer?

Only failure: knees touching the ground or your torso dropping to the floor. The clock freezes accurate to the frame — no grace seconds, no cheating. Raise a hand to the camera and it resets for the next round.

Is my video saved anywhere?

No. Frames are analyzed live in memory and discarded instantly. Nothing is recorded, nothing is uploaded, and the only thing persisted is your hold history — stored locally on your device.

Which phones does it work on?

Any Android phone from 7.0 (Nougat) onward with a front camera. Newer phones get faster inference thanks to GPU acceleration, but it runs everywhere.

Why isn't it on the Play Store?

It ships here first, direct and unencumbered. Verify the SHA-256 checksum above after downloading if you want certainty the file is untouched.
